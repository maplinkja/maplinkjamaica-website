Review the most common questions from Jamaican business owners.

What is a Google Business Profile?

A free business listing that can display your contact information, hours, services, photos, reviews, and directions on Google Search and Google Maps.

Why does my business need one?

A complete and accurate profile makes it easier for customers to find, contact, and learn about your business.

Do I need a physical location?

Not always. Eligible service-area businesses can serve customers without displaying a public storefront, subject to Google policies.

How long does setup take?

Work begins after we receive the required information. Google verification and review times vary by business and verification method.

Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No. We use responsible best practices, but no provider can honestly guarantee a specific ranking.

What information do I need to provide?

Business name, category, phone, email, address or service area, hours, logo, photos, and services.

Can you update an existing profile?

Yes. We can improve information, map pins, descriptions, categories, photos, and other profile details.

Do you serve all of Jamaica?

Yes. We serve businesses across all 14 parishes.

What happens after I purchase a package?

You will receive instructions to complete our business information form. We then begin the agreed work and keep you informed.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You may upgrade as your business grows.
