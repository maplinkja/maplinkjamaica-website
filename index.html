Frequently asked questions

Questions business owners often ask

Clear answers before you get started.

What is a Google Business Profile?

It is the business listing that can appear in Google Search and Google Maps with your business name, phone number, hours, location, photos, reviews, and services.

Do I need a physical storefront?

No. Eligible service-area businesses can hide their address and show the areas they serve instead, subject to Google’s current eligibility rules.

Can MapLink JA guarantee first place on Google?

No legitimate provider can guarantee a specific ranking. We improve profile completeness, accuracy, relevance, and local visibility using responsible optimization practices.

How long does setup take?

The preparation work can usually be completed quickly once all information is received. Google verification and review times vary depending on the method offered and the profile.

What information will I need to provide?

You will need your official business details, contact information, business category, hours, address or service areas, services, logo, and suitable business photos.
