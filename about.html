Who we are

A Jamaican digital visibility company

MapLink JA is dedicated to helping businesses strengthen their online presence through Google Business Profile optimization and local search solutions.

Customers often search online before deciding where to shop, eat, or request services. We help businesses across Jamaica become easier to find quickly, accurately, and confidently.

Why MapLink JA Started

MapLink JA was founded after recognizing that many Jamaican businesses offer excellent products and services but struggle to be found online.

We believe every business—regardless of size—deserves the opportunity to be discovered by potential customers. Digital visibility should be accessible, professional, and focused on local growth.
